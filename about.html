Project Overview

WOREX LTD (World Resources Exchange) is a premium integrated agribusiness enterprise founded to revolutionize livestock and aquaculture production in Rwanda. We combine animal feed production, pig fattening, and fish farming into a seamless, climate-smart operation that delivers exceptional products and sustainable value.

Our Story

With over 25 years of experience in agricultural innovation, WOREX LTD was established to address Rwanda's growing demand for high-quality protein and animal feed. Our commitment to sustainability and excellence has made us a trusted partner for farmers, traders, and institutions across the nation.

Mission

To deliver affordable, high-quality animal feeds and protein products through efficient and sustainable production systems that empower farming communities and strengthen Rwanda's food security.

Vision

To become the leading integrated livestock and aquaculture enterprise in East Africa, recognized for innovation, sustainability, and customer excellence while supporting Rwanda's green growth objectives.

Core Values

  • Sustainability: Circular economy practices minimize environmental impact
